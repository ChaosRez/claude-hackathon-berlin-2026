    Graph typology

    How shapes are assigned

    Zigzag: the answer switches between disagreeing and supporting at least twice. Spiral: it reinforces earlier points two or more times and still reaches depth 4. Circle: it ends by coming back to the question or its opening claim and stays within depth 3. Hub and spoke: one point has three or more children and nothing goes deeper than 3. Tree: two or more points branch. Line: most entities build on the one just before. Anything else is a wandering path.

    Entity type mix

    How entities are cut

    Each answer is split into sentences, then split again at a semicolon or at a comma followed by but, however, although, though, whereas or yet. A list number such as "2." stays with the sentence after it. The labeller can merge a piece into the one before it when it does not carry its own unit of meaning. Each resulting entity gets one type, an optional parent it builds on, and optional earlier entities it repeats.
