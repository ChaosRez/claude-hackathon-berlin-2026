What “better” means

1 · Accurate

Matches graphs made by people

Same main claim, same parent for each piece, same support or attack.

2 · Stable

Same answer, same graph

Run twice, the graphs agree. Noise would blur any human-vs-ChatGPT difference.

3 · Valid and cheap

Passes our rules, at low cost

Every graph checked against the schemas; dollars per answer.

Order matters: accuracy first, then stability, then cost.

We compare approaches on three things, in this order. Stability matters for our research question: if two runs on the same answer differ, a small difference between human and ChatGPT graphs could be noise.
